## Acedia

Say what’s on your mind. Acedia turns voice notes into task cards with titles, priorities, and deadlines. Review and organize them in a list or on a Kanban board.

| Capture | Organize | Follow through |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictate a quick reminder or a longer brain dump. | Turn it into structured tasks and group related work. | See what’s next and what’s due on your board. |

Start with the [Acedia Docs](https://docs.acedianote.com) for help with projects, boards, voice input, or integrations.
